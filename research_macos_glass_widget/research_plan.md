# Research Plan: macOS Liquid Glass Widget Boilerplates

## Main Research Question
Find high-adoption, highly-praised macOS widget/menu bar boilerplate repositories or design templates that implement modern "liquid glass" (glassmorphism, vibrant backdrop-blur/vibrancy, modern SwiftUI UI) styling.

## Subtopics for Investigation
1. **macOS SwiftUI Menu Bar / MenuBarExtra Boilerplates**: High adoption repositories that provide templates for menu bar popovers.
2. **macOS Glassmorphic / Liquid UI Boilerplates & Libraries**: Libraries or design systems demonstrating premium glassmorphism, translucency, or vibrant UI layouts natively on macOS.

## Synthesis Strategy
Compare the discovered repositories based on:
- Adoption metrics (GitHub stars, forks).
- Visual aesthetics (suitability for "liquid glass" theme, vibrancy/blur support).
- Development readiness (easy boilerplate integration for macOS Tahoe).
- Community reputation and praise.
